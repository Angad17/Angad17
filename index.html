About Dripzy

More than clothes. A lifestyle.

Dripzy started in Hyderabad in 2024 with one idea: give people a way to wear what they actually feel. Not what a brand decided looked good this season. Your art, your energy, your statement — printed on premium heavyweights built to last.

We run a mobile printing setup so you can walk up and get it done on the spot. This site brings that same experience online — upload your design, see it live on the shirt, order it to your door. Clothes for a higher state of mind.
